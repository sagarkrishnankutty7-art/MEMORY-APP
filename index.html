Chess Luxe

New to chess? Learn the pieces and rules

Goal: trap the opponent's king so it has no escape (checkmate). White (gold) moves first, then you take turns. Moving onto an enemy piece captures it.

  • Check: your king is attacked. You must move it, block, or capture the attacker.
  • Castling: move the king two squares toward a rook and the rook jumps over it, if neither has moved and the squares between are empty and safe.
  • En passant: a pawn can capture a pawn that just moved two squares, as if it had moved one.
  • Promotion: a pawn reaching the far end becomes a queen.
  • Colours: green marks are moves; orange marks are moves where the opponent could capture you. Blue squares are the hint.
